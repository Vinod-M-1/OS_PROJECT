Page Replacement Theory & Analytics

Complete conceptual breakdown from real-world intuition to algorithmic benchmarks and Belady's Anomaly proofs.

1. The Real-World Study Desk Analogy

Imagine studying at a small desk that only holds 3 books at once, while your bookshelf across the room stores hundreds of books:

  • Page Hit: You need a book and it is already sitting on your desk—instant access with zero delay.
  • Page Fault: The book is not on your desk. You must walk to the bookshelf, retrieve it, and bring it back (slow disk I/O).
  • Eviction Policy: When your desk is full, you must choose which book to return to the shelf to make space.
